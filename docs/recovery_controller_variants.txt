M2 (peer-review) — Recovery time by controller variant (honest attribution)
===========================================================================
Reviewer M2: the paper's headline "40 ms recovery" is the EVENT-DRIVEN controller
(reroute_agent.py, port-status triggered). The paper's titular RL system
(self_healing_agent.py, PPO decides from telemetry) recovers only as fast as its
3 s telemetry poll -- ~2 orders of magnitude slower. Report BOTH and attribute the
40 ms correctly.

Measured with topology/measure_recovery.py, single mid-link fault (s2-s3), 20 ms
probe resolution, on the identical topology.

VERIFIED RESULTS (2026-07-01)
-----------------------------
Controller variant                          | recovery time      | what bounds it
--------------------------------------------|--------------------|-------------------------
Event-driven reroute (reroute_agent.py)     | 40 +/- 13 ms  (5c) | OF port-status event -> immediate OFPFlowMod
PPO-driven (self_healing_agent.py)          | 3400 +/- 633 ms(3c)| 3 s telemetry poll interval (POLL_SECONDS=3)
OpenFlow fast-failover (failover_agent.py)  | 52 +/- 10 ms (node)| data-plane liveness (node fault only; see M1)

Per-cycle PPO outages: 3700, 2520, 3980 ms (tx=550 rx=365/424/351 at 20 ms spacing).

Interpretation (honest, for the paper):
- The 40 ms headline is the EVENT-DRIVEN controller. It must be attributed as such;
  it is NOT the recovery time of the PPO decision path.
- The PPO controller recovers in ~3.4 s because detection is bounded by the 3 s
  OFPPortStats poll: a failed primary port must read zero throughput on a poll
  before PPO can decide to reroute. The actuation itself (OFPFlowMod) is still
  instant; the latency is entirely the telemetry cadence.
- This reinforces the paper's central honest finding: the PPO agent is not what
  makes recovery fast (and simple classifiers already match its detection). The
  fast, deterministic path is the event-driven controller. PPO is best presented
  as an ablation showing the RL framing adds latency without accuracy benefit
  (feeds M3 retitle and M5 "demote PPO to ablation").
- Lowering POLL_SECONDS would cut PPO recovery time but raises control-plane
  overhead; an event-driven + PPO hybrid (event triggers, PPO confirms) is future
  work.
